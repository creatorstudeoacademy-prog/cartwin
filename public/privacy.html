CartWin Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

CartWin ("we", "our") is a Shopify app that displays a discount popup to shoppers who are about to abandon their cart.

Data we collect

  • Shop domain and access tokens: Required for OAuth authentication and API access. Stored securely.
  • Popup settings: Your configured discount code, colors, text, and timing preferences.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect customer personal data (names, emails, addresses).
  • We do not track shoppers across stores.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Data retention

When you uninstall CartWin, all your shop data is deleted within 48 hours via Shopify's mandatory webhooks.
